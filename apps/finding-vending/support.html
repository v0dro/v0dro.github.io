Finding Vending

Finding Vending maps vending machines in Japan. Take a photo of one, rate it, and it goes on the map for everyone else.

Common questions

My machine has not appeared on the map

Every upload is checked automatically before it is published, which normally takes a second or two. If the check is unsure, a person reviews it — that can take longer. A photo that does not show a vending machine is rejected.

The photo would not upload

Uploads continue in the background and retry by themselves, so a weak signal is not a problem — leave the app and it will finish. Location must be switched on: a machine cannot be placed without coordinates.

Something on the map is wrong or offensive

Tap the machine, then Report or send feedback. Reports about offensive content, privacy, or something that is not a vending machine take the machine off the map immediately, until a person has looked at it.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your profile picture at the top right of the map, then Delete account. This removes your account and every machine you submitted. It cannot be undone. If you have already uninstalled the app, email us a deletion request instead — the link opens a message already addressed and filled in.

Still stuck?

Write to us and include what you were doing and roughly when — it helps us find the request in the logs.
