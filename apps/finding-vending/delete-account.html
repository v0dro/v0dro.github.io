Account Deletion Request

Finding Vending (Find My Drink Japan) · Data & account removal

Use this page to delete your Finding Vending account and the data tied to it: your sign-in record, and every machine submission (photo, location, notes, rating) you have shared. Deleted submissions are removed from the public map.

Only want to remove what’s on your phone? Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases the local upload queue, pending photos and your sign-in session. Use this page if you also want your account and shared submissions deleted from the server.

Request deletion

Tell us how you sign in so we can find your account. Submitting the form opens your email client with the request pre-filled, addressed to the developer.

Leave blank for an anonymous account, and instead describe one of your submissions below (roughly where and when) so we can locate it.

What happens next

  • Your email client opens with a pre-filled message to the developer.
  • Send the email to complete the request.
  • Your account and/or submissions are deleted within 30 days: the Firebase sign-in record, the photos and metadata on the photo server, and the entries in the public machine index.
  • You’ll receive a confirmation reply once the deletion is done.

Server backups that may contain your data are rotated out within a further 30 days. Nothing other than your submissions and sign-in record is kept about you.

Prefer to email directly?

Send your request to sameer.deshmukh93@gmail.com with the sign-in method and email address you use in the app.
