Privacy Policy

Finding Vending · effective 27 August 2026

Finding Vending is a crowdsourced map of vending machines in Japan. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What the app collects

  • Photos you take of a vending machine, and only those. The app never reads your photo library.
  • The location of the machine — GPS coordinates captured when you take the photo, sent with the submission and written into the image file. This is the point of the app: without coordinates a machine cannot go on the map.
  • What you type about a machine: a 0–5 rating, free-text notes and the payment methods it accepts.
  • An account identifier. Every submission is signed by a Firebase account. By default that account is anonymous — a random identifier with no personal information attached. If you choose to sign in with Google, Apple or an email address, we additionally hold the email address and display name that provider gives us.
  • Reports you send through the in-app report button: the machine identifier, your account identifier, the reason and your comment.
  • Technical diagnostics. When a step inside the app fails — the camera returns no photo, a crop cannot be saved, an upload is rejected — the app records a short, fixed-format note and sends it to our own server in the background. Each note holds the name of the step that failed, the time, your app version, your Android or iOS version, your phone's model name, and a handful of numbers such as a file size or a status code, signed with the same account identifier as your submissions. It never contains free text, photo contents, filenames, file paths, coordinates, or anything you typed. Without these notes a failure that happens on your phone is invisible to us and we cannot fix it.

Before a photo leaves your phone the app strips the EXIF tags that identify the device or its owner — camera make and model, serial numbers, software version, owner name. The location tags and the image orientation are deliberately kept.

What the app does not collect

  • No contacts, calendars, microphone, health data or advertising identifier.
  • No background location. Location is used only while the app is open.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs. Nothing on your phone reports to Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry or anything similar; the technical diagnostics above go only to our own server.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

What other people can see

When a submission is approved, other users see the machine's coordinates, your rating, your notes and the payment methods — nothing else. Your account identifier, your email address and the photograph itself are never sent to other users' devices. The photo is used only for automated and human review of the submission.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase — authentication and the map database.
  • Google Maps — map tiles.
  • Cloudflare — the network path to our server.

Photographs are stored on our own server, not in third-party cloud storage.

How long it is kept

Machine data stays as long as the machine is on the map. Deleted submissions are moved to a holding area and removed permanently on a retention schedule; encrypted backups may retain a copy for a limited period after that.

Diagnostic notes are deleted when you delete your account.

Deleting your account

Open the app, tap your profile picture at the top right, then Delete account. This permanently removes your sign-in account and every machine you submitted, including any still awaiting review. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot reach the app — you have already uninstalled it, or the button fails — email us a deletion request and we will do it for you. That link opens a message already addressed and filled in; just add the details it asks for. We answer to confirm once the account is gone.

Children

Finding Vending is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the effective date above.
