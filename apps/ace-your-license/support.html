Ace Your License: Japan

Ace Your License: Japan is a practice game for the Japanese driving test course. You drive a car on a top-down map of the course, and an examiner scores you as the real test would. It is an unofficial practice tool: it is not affiliated with any licensing authority and does not replace the official test.

Common questions

Which routes are free?

Route 1 (外免切替 普通1号, the foreign licence conversion route) is always free, and it is the route the How to play tour uses. Every other route is ¥480 on its own, or you can unlock them all with a monthly subscription (¥980 a month) or a single payment (¥4,890). Locked routes can still be selected and previewed; the button shows UNLOCK when you tap it.

I bought a route but it is still locked

Tap Restore purchases. You will find it on the start screen and in the unlock sheet. The app checks your purchases again whenever it comes back to the front, so it can also take a moment after a purchase. Restoring uses the account you bought with: the Apple ID on iPhone or iPad, or the Google account on Android. If you bought on a different account, sign in with that one in the store first.

How do I redeem a code?

  • iPhone and iPad: tap Redeem code in the unlock sheet. Apple's offer-code sheet opens; enter the code there, then tap Restore purchases in the app.
  • Android: redeem a Google Play promo code in the Play Store app (profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Redeem code), or tap Redeem code in the unlock sheet and paste it. The app picks the code up when it comes back to the front.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iPhone and iPad: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Ace Your License: Japan. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the paid period ends.
  • Android: open the Play Store app, go to profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel from there.

I want a refund

Purchases are handled by the store, not by the app, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play order help at support.google.com/googleplay. If you are not sure which one applies, write to us and we will point you the right way.

The car will not move

The car starts in P (Park). Tap D on the gear selector, then hold GAS. Pressing GAS while in P shows a reminder and flashes the selector.

I got lost or the car is off the road

Tap ↺ Track to put the car back on the route, facing along it and stopped. To begin the route again, tap Restart. Both are in the ☰ menu and on the driving screen.

I do not understand a deduction

The result card lists every deduction with the number of the action it refers to. The How to play tour in the ☰ menu explains the signals, mirror and look checks, and stops. Easy and Medium leave out some checks, and Reality scores every rule. The level is chosen on the start screen and can be changed there between drives.

Something is wrong or confusing in the app

Open the ☰ menu and tap Send feedback, or use "How was this drive?" on the result card. You can attach the drive's route log so we can see what happened. Nothing is sent until you tap send. If the route you want is missing, the search results end with "Not here? Request a centre", which sends us the request the same way.

Still stuck?

Write to us and include the route you were driving, the device and store you bought with, and roughly when it happened. It helps us find the problem quickly.
