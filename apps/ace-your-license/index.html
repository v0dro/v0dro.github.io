Learn the course by driving it

Every drive is on the course itself. The map follows the printed test route, with the lanes, kerbs, stop lines, railway crossings, traffic lights and bridges drawn as they are on the ground. Choose a route, then drive it with an on-screen steering wheel, gas and brake pedals and a P / D / R gear selector.

What the examiner checks

Signals and mirrors

Signal before every turn and lane change, check the mirrors, and look over your shoulder before you move.

Stops

Full stops at とまれ lines, railway crossings, hill starts on the bridges and the exits of the crank and S-curve.

Careful driving

Stay on the road. Touching a kerb costs points, and going through a light on red ends the exam.

Clear feedback

The result card lists every deduction with the place it happened, so you know exactly what to practise next.

Three levels

Start with Easy, which only scores your signals, then move up to Medium, which adds the stops and lights, or Reality, which scores every rule. The level is yours to choose on the start screen.

Built for practice

  • A short guided tour in the menu shows how to drive, signal, check and stop.
  • Hints show the next thing to do, and can be switched off once you know the course.
  • Choose your camera view and zoom, from close up to the whole course, with a mini map.
  • Search the routes by kanji, kana or romaji.
