Privacy Policy

Ace Your License: Japan · effective 1 November 2026

Ace Your License: Japan is a practice game for the Japanese driving test course. You drive a car on a top-down map of the test course and an examiner scores you. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and how to get rid of it. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone or tablet

The following is kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent anywhere by the app:

  • Settings: the difficulty level, camera view and zoom, the hints switch, and whether you have seen the tours.
  • Unlock state: which paid routes you have bought or unlocked, so the app still works offline.
  • Unsent feedback: if a feedback report cannot be sent straight away, it waits on the device until it can be sent or you delete the app.

What is sent, and only when you ask

The app sends data only when you choose to send feedback. Nothing is sent during a drive.

  • Feedback you send: the star ratings, the chips you tick, any text you type, the app version and the platform. If you leave "Attach this drive's route log" on, the report also includes a log of that drive: the car's position, heading and speed, the route you drove, and the scoring events. The log holds no name, no account and no location outside the course.
  • Where it goes: to the developer by one of three routes. If a feedback server is configured in the build, the report is posted to it over HTTPS. Otherwise your mail app opens with a draft addressed to the developer, and you choose whether to send it. If neither is possible, the report stays on your device.
  • Requesting a centre: if you use "Request a centre", the search text and any note you write are sent the same way as feedback.

Purchases

Paid routes are sold through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. Payment is handled entirely by the store; the app never sees your card or account details. The store tells the app which products you own. Google and Apple have their own privacy policies covering the purchase itself.

Unlock codes are checked on the device against a signature built into the app. Entering one sends nothing.

Store review prompt

After a passed route the app may ask whether you would like to rate it. The rating prompt is provided by Google Play or Apple. The app does not see what you rate or write.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • It does not create an account or ask for your name or email address.
  • It does not show advertising and does not use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Children

The app is a practice tool for adults preparing for the driving test. It is not directed at children, and the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings and unlock state are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Feedback that has already been sent is held by the developer; to ask for it to be deleted, email the address below with the date you sent it.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting something new, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before the update is released.
